Sinigang

Classic soup of the philippines

Facts about sinigang:

  • Sinigang is a popular Filipino soup known for its sour and savory taste.
  • It is usually made with tamarind, which gives the soup its sour flavor.
  • Common meats used are pork, beef, shrimp, fish, or chicken.

Sinigang recipe:

Ingridients:

  • 1 kg pork belly (or pork ribs)
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 packet sinigang mix (or fresh tamarind)
  • 1 radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 10 pieces string bean
  • 2 cups kangkong (water spinach)
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Boil water in a large pot.
  2. Add the pork and cook until it becomes tender.
  3. Add the onion and tomatoes, then let it simmer.
  4. Add the radish, eggplant, and string beans.
  5. Mix in the sinigang seasoning or tamarind extract.
  6. Season with fish sauce, salt, and pepper.
  7. Add the kangkong and green chili peppers.
  8. Cook for a few more minutes until the vegetables are done.
  9. Serve hot with steamed rice.
