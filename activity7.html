 Cool down with our refreshing Halo-Halo, a beloved Filipino dessert made with layers of sweetened fruits, beans, jellies, nata de coco, and shaved ice, topped with creamy evaporated milk. Finished with a scoop of rich ube ice cream and a sprinkle of crunchy toppings, every spoonful offers a delicious mix of flavors and textures. It's the perfect sweet treat to enjoy on a warm day or after a satisfying meal.
